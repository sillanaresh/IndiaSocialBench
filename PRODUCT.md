# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

IndiaSocialBench is for researchers and product teams at Sarvam and other Indic model labs, model builders comparing cultural and language behavior, and enterprise teams evaluating assistants for Indian users.

## Purpose

IndiaSocialBench measures how language models respond to emotionally difficult Indian conversations. It uses original scenarios in English, Hinglish, and Hindi, then publishes model, language, and dimension results with the transcripts and judge explanations behind them.

The product succeeds when its findings are credible, easy to inspect, and useful for improving models.

## Brand Personality

IndiaSocialBench is rigorous, candid, and culturally grounded.

## Anti-References

Avoid generic AI and software marketing, decorative tricolor or mandala motifs, exaggerated claims, hidden methods, fabricated data, and cultural stereotypes.

## Principles

1. Show evidence before making a claim.
2. Put the leaderboard and transcripts before promotional language.
3. Be culturally specific without treating any norm as universal.
4. State limitations and incomplete validation clearly.
5. Use a restrained visual system that keeps attention on the research.

## Accessibility

The site must work well on mobile, support keyboard navigation, respect reduced motion, meet WCAG AA contrast, and render Devanagari clearly.
